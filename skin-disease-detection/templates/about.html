Our Mission

Our goal is to make skincare awareness more accessible through technology. By combining AI and dermatological insights, we help users learn about skin conditions and take sensible preventive measures.

How the Site Works

  • 🖼️ Upload your skin image — the AI analyzes it using deep learning.
  • 📋 Get instant results with condition descriptions and suggestions.
  • 📊 Generate downloadable PDF reports.
  • 📅 Book dermatologist appointments or find nearby clinics.
  • 💬 Chat with our AI assistant for skincare guidance and FAQs.

Technology Behind the App

A MobileNet model fine-tuned with transfer learning on the HAM10000 dataset using TensorFlow and Keras. The frontend uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript; the backend is Flask (Python) with OpenCV for image processing, SQLite for storage and FPDF for reports. Chat is powered via the OpenRouter API.

Our Project Journey

Starting as a college project, AI SkinCare grew into a full web app through continuous experimentation: training models, handling class imbalance, and designing a friendly interface.

⚠️ For educational and research use only. Not for medical diagnosis.
